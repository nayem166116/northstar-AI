# Image slots

Replace any file with your own artwork at the same path and filename. Every image is a CSS background with `background-size:cover`, so keep the aspect ratio close to the original.

## Where the images come from

- **Founder portraits on the home hero** are the real founders where they publish a photo: Saphira AI, Zapdos Labs, Mineflow, PatternFast, Truffle AI. Sources are Y Combinator company pages and one company press release. Confirm usage rights before a public launch.
- **Callie Care and ClaimQI** publish no founder photo anywhere public, so those two cards use a licensed stock portrait.
- **Everything else** uses free-license Pexels photography chosen to match the sector or article topic (factory, warehouse, data centre, grid, healthcare, finance, fashion studio, mining, developer, university). Pexels photos are free for commercial use with no attribution required.
- **Team and fellows photos** are licensed stock portraits, because those profiles are written as examples and the people are not real.
- Hero and caption slots have a soft dark gradient baked into the JPEG so the white overlay text stays readable. Keep that in mind when you swap a file.
